Getting started

Three ways to your first training plan.

No account needed on first launch. Helpanion shows you how to get going – with a short explanation of each option.

  • With your own AI: a few questions, Helpanion writes the request for your AI
  • From a file: import a plan in Helpanion format, e.g. from your coach
  • Sample plan: try it right away and delete it any time later
During training

One glance, one tap – keep going.

Helpanion shows exactly what you need between two sets: exercise, target reps, intensity and your rest. Large buttons, one-handed use, the screen stays on.

  • Before the first set: your machine setup and the weight from last time
  • Rest timer that keeps running when you leave the app
  • Weight and reps with a tap, corrections at any time
  • Weight suggestion from your 1RM, a calibration set while it is still unknown
  • Machine empty weight is added automatically, weights in kg or lb
  • Skip an exercise or pick an alternative
  • Add an exercise on the spot and change the order – your plan stays unchanged
  • Mark warm-up sets with one tap – they do not count as work sets
  • New record? Helpanion tells you right when you log the set
  • Crash-safe: every set is saved instantly
Wear OS

Your phone can stay in your pocket.

Your watch shows the exercise, the target and your rest. When the rest is over, it vibrates – phone and watch stay in sync.

  • Remaining rest time, big and easy to read
  • Confirm sets and adjust weight, reps and RPE right on your wrist
  • The weight from last time before the first set
  • Vibration when your rest ends
  • Records and warm-up sets visible on your wrist too
Plan with your own AI

Your plan, built with the AI of your choice.

A few questions about goal, experience, time and equipment – Helpanion turns them into a ready-made request for ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or another AI. Paste the answer, check the preview and start training.

  • Hints and recommendations matching your experience
  • Follow-up plan: your progress and your rating are taken into account
  • Sex optional – for load management and tone
  • If you want, an AI with memory may use what it already knows about you
  • The AI assigns muscle groups to every exercise
  • Your answers stay on your device
Planning and progress

Remember what you pressed last week? Helpanion does.

Plans with weeks, phases and progression. Your history shows how long ago your last training was, when the recommended rest is over and what you achieved.

  • Weeks and sessions at a glance, progression per week
  • Start your next training with one tap
  • Sets and total weight moved per session
  • Exercise history with records and e1RM trend, target vs. actual
  • Sets per muscle group – as bars or on the body map
  • Exercise library: maintain muscle groups, 1RM and video per exercise, add with AI
  • With an Oura ring: your readiness against your daily form
Your data
